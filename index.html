Gesture guide
Move the cursor
Raise your index finger and keep the others curled.
Click and hold
With your index raised, pinch your thumb and pinky together. Release to let go.
Double click
Lower your index finger to half height and pinch.
Right click
Curl your index finger down and pinch.
Scroll
Raise index and middle fingers, then move up or down. Hold near the top or bottom edge to keep scrolling.
Volume and tracks
Raise index, middle and ring fingers. Move up or down for volume, swipe left or right to change track.

Touchpad

One finger moves. Tap to click.
To select: tap twice, but keep your finger down on the 2nd tap and move.
Two fingers scroll or pinch to zoom.

Touchpad gestures
Move
Slide one finger. Slow movements are precise, fast ones travel further.
Click and double click
Tap once to click. Tap twice quickly to double click.
Select text or drag
Double-tap and move: tap once, then touch again straight away, keep your finger down on that second touch and slide. The text is selected as you move. Lift to finish. For long drags use Drag lock, or hold Left click with one thumb and move with another finger.
Right click
Tap with two fingers, or use the Right click button.
Middle click
Tap with three fingers.
Scroll
Slide two fingers up, down, left or right.
Zoom
Pinch two fingers together or apart (sends Ctrl + scroll).
Three-finger swipe
Up for Task view, down for Show desktop, left or right for Alt + Tab.
